# Construction authority: one validator for every deck-construction verdict

Accepted for `mcp-construction-authority-consolidation` under the [engine charter](../ENGINE-CHARTER.md) (deck-construction legality and policy belong to the existing MCP validation services; admission reuses that authority and no second validator may exist). The unit was split from [catalog objects](engine-mvp-catalog-objects.md) (`think:407`); the research finding and design are `think:421`. Native Magistr records hold execution status. No engine source, contract, protocol or gameplay route changed; no verdict moved to Rust.

## Scope

The public `validateDeck` composition (`validate_deck`, `deck_status`, the `analyze_deck` vitals, the deck plan, construction and engine capture all compose the same three rule modules) and the deck-plan, deck-construction and build-construction guards disagreed on four verdicts: a card whose Commander legality is `not_legal` passed `validateDeck` while construction refused it; a commander also in the 99 and a companion inside the deck passed `validateDeck` while construction reported overlaps; an unmodeled or heuristic companion passed `validateDeck` silently while three private copies of a characteristics predicate marked it unresolved in construction; and a legendary card that is a creature card in every zone but the battlefield had no eligibility reading and no audit case. Capture refuses construction errors; this unit settles what counts as one.

## Ownership and reuse review

- **One owner per verdict.** Every `legalities.commander` read outside `coreRules.ts` is gone; every companion-characteristics predicate outside `companionRules.ts` is gone; the construction paths map a violation's rule to their code and choose only the construction severity. Identical rule and code names were chosen over a discriminator field on `Violation` because a distinct rule is already branchable without prose parsing.
- **Reused seams.** `copyLimit` decides the command-zone overlap exactly as it decides the singleton rule; `companionRuleCoverage` and the existing `COMPANION_RULES` table decide resolvability; the `commanderCharacteristics` front-face reading governs the new eligibility text as it governs the others; the census reuses `isCommanderEligible` unchanged.
- **Overlap removed.** Three copies of the characteristics predicate, three legality guards in `deck/construction.ts`, one in `deckPlan.ts`, one in `build/construction.ts`, and the seed-presence and commander-presence overlap checks. Overlap retained deliberately: the deck plan reports a consolidated code once per view (normalization judges the seed inventory, the plan judges the whole deck) because both views were already reported before this unit.
- **Alternatives rejected.** Severity `warning` for an unresolved condition (capture would admit an uncertified companion deck); evaluating a heuristic restriction and reporting its breaks (a changeling in a Kaheera deck produced a false conflict in the first draft); extending the frozen excerpt with CR 604.3 for the Grist case (the digest binding would move for a case 903.3 and 101.1 already support).
- **Boundary kept.** The Rust engine owns runtime state only; capture refuses the new errors through the unchanged `validateDeck` call.

## Rules basis

From the frozen 20260819 excerpt (effective 2026-08-07): 100.2c and 903.5 (Commander decks are subject to the format's deck construction rules; the legal pool and the banned list are Scryfall data), 903.5a and 903.5b (the commander counts among the 100; each card has a different English name unless its own text says otherwise under 101.1), 702.139a (the companion is one card owned from outside the game whose condition the starting deck must fulfil), 903.3 and 101.1 (a creature card is eligible; a card whose own text makes it a creature card in every zone but the battlefield is read outside the battlefield).

## Migration

The public `validate_deck` output changed; consumers reviewed:

- `validate_deck`, `deck_status`, `analyze_deck` and engine capture: a deck that passed only because of one of the four gaps now fails; a Kaheera or Zirda deck is no longer `ok`.
- `deck_set_companion`: `condition_met` is false for an unresolved condition.
- `validate_card` and `deck_add`: a `not_legal` card is refused before it is added (`deck_add force:true` still applies it).
- Deck plan and construction: the five codes are unchanged, their messages are now the validator's; a banned mandatory card is `BANLIST` (was `FORMAT_ILLEGAL`); a heuristic companion no longer yields per-card `COMPANION` conflicts beside the unresolved diagnostic; the build library excludes candidates on any companion violation.
- Card search: `is:commander` reads the ingest flag, which admits Grist after the next `data_ingest`; the validator is authoritative today.

Recorded in `CHANGELOG.md` under 0.3.0.

## Limits

- Kaheera's and Zirda's restrictions remain heuristic and are reported unresolved rather than evaluated; exact evaluation needs face-aware creature types and an activated-ability owner and stays with the validator when that evidence exists.
- The served `is_commander_eligible` column reflects the ingest heuristic at ingest time; it lags the validator until the next `data_ingest`.
- The deck plan and construction report a consolidated code once per view; clients that count diagnostics see two entries for a seed-and-plan overlap, as before.
- `checkCommanderLibraryOverlap` reads the copy limit; a bounded grant whose numeral is not understood is one copy, matching the singleton rule.
