## What was found

The core needed no change. After the split units, setup allocates two to four seats, every loss path runs the one leave-the-game owner, combat and opponent scope are per living opponent, and the loop drops a departed player's pending action. Seven integrated scenarios confirm that at four seats, and four mutants show the scenarios would notice a regression.

The only thing stopping a four-seat game through the worker and MCP was the authored contract: `PreviewSeat` spanned seats 0–1 and `PreviewScenario.seats` held exactly two entries, with a protocol fixture asserting that four preview seats are invalid. The worker's trial driver and the engine's preview setup were already seat-count general.

## Ownership and reuse review (closing)

One owner per decision holds across the four units:

- **Seats and setup:** `setup::build_game` / `prepare_preview_game`. The worker and the conformance fixtures both call it; there is no seat-zero shortcut and no second multiplayer setup.
- **Leaving the game:** `mtg_engine::elimination`. Concession, zero life, poison and empty-library draws all arrive there; the loop only logs and updates its priority round.
- **Turn and APNAP order, living opponents:** `TurnManager` and `GameState::living_opponents`.
- **Priority:** `PriorityManager`; an action resets the succession of passes, departure removes the seat from the live round.
- **Combat membership:** `GameState::reconcile_control` calling the one `CombatState` removal primitive.
- **Wire shape:** the authored `contract.json`; Rust DTOs, validators and the TypeScript Zod schemas are generated. `PREVIEW_MIN_SEATS`/`PREVIEW_MAX_SEATS` in the MCP service mirror the generated bound for the tool's input schema; the worker contract remains the authority and refuses anything else as a malformed frame.

No TypeScript gameplay logic was added. The MCP tests assert the worker's outcome; they do not compute one.

Duplicate paths still present, with their removal milestones (unchanged by this chunk): the legacy TypeScript simulation behind `simulate_deck` goes at `engine-mvp-cutover`; the two-seat match, store and rating harness (`MatchSpec`, `mtg-cli rate`) is generalized by `engine-eval-multiplayer-matches`.

## Rules basis

Wizards' 2026-08-07 Comprehensive Rules as pinned with the fixture: 117.3b–d and 117.4 (who receives priority, and resolution only after all players pass in succession), 608.2b (a spell whose every target is illegal does not resolve), 104.3a (concession), 800.4a (a leaving player's objects, spells and abilities), 800.4j–k (a departed active player's turn), 101.4 (turn order), 103.8a and 103.8c (first-turn draw, see limits).

## Evidence

Engine, `tests/multiplayer_conformance.rs` (seven scenarios, all on unchanged production code):

1. An object resolves only after all four seats pass, in turn order.
2. A non-active caster keeps priority and the succession restarts from them.
3. A survivor's spell aimed at a player who then leaves goes to its owner's graveyard and changes nothing.
4. A controller who leaves with a resolution choice pending: the choice ceases with the spell, another player's ability below it still resolves, and the departed seat is never asked again.
5. A concession with two abilities on the stack removes only the conceder's.
6. Turn order closes over a conceded next seat and a conceding active seat (turns 2–5: seats 2, 3, 0, 2).
7. A four-seat fixture game through `prepare_preview_game` ends with one survivor after three eliminations and replays to the same state hash and log.

## Limits

- **The fixture's prose still says "two-seat".** The internal fixture is digest-pinned and was not edited; seat count is a property of the scenario. Earlier receipts that call the preview two-seat describe their own revision.
- **First-turn draw.** Games built through setup start in the first seat's first main phase, so that seat never takes a turn-one draw. That matches CR 103.8a for two players and not 103.8c for three or four. It is why seat 0 outlasts a passing pod. Recorded on `engine-mvp-mulligans`, which owns the starting procedure.
- **Passes are not logged.** The order in which seats received priority cannot be recovered from a game log. Recorded on `engine-mvp-replay`.
- **Concession is an action at priority.** A player cannot concede in the middle of a resolution, so a pending choice can only lose its chooser through the state-level owner today; scenario 4 exercises that path directly.
- **Policies.** The baseline sends every attacker at the next living seat and never blocks; four-seat policy strength is unmeasured (`engine-eval-multiplayer-matches`, `engine-mvp-policies`).
- **Still the internal fixture.** No authored deck, commander, command zone or 40-life Commander pod runs through MCP yet; that is `engine-mvp-commander` and the chunks after it. The worker's `--self-check` still builds a two-player state.
- Each-opponent effect vocabulary, the remaining causes of CR 506.4 and full damage assignment stay on `engine-mvp-effects`, `engine-mvp-layers` / `engine-any-combat-families` and `engine-combat-damage-assignment`.
