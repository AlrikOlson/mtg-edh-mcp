# Independent pods: one authored deck version per seat

Accepted for `engine-any-pod-inputs` under the [engine charter](../ENGINE-CHARTER.md). The research finding and plan are `think:435` and `think:436`; native Magistr records hold execution status. It generalizes the [Commander admission](engine-mvp-commander.md), which bound every seat to the one captured deck, to a pod of independently authored deck versions.

The [source pin](../../engine/source.json) advances to canonical `mtg-game-engine` commit `d8f53de8d25a49ebcf4fe9649fc264b34685d676` on `main`. The root `Cargo.lock` is unchanged. The worker contract moves to protocol 7, so `contract.json`, `fixtures.json`, both generated bindings, the synced README and the manifest change together.

## Scope

Before this unit the Commander admission's catalog was one capture and `mtg_worker::commander::bind` required every seat's `deck_digest` and the scenario's `snapshot_digest` to equal that capture's digest: a pod could only be a mirror. The MCP side captured one authored deck version at a time and built no Commander admission in production. This unit makes the admission's catalog a pod of per-deck captures, binds each seat to its own capture, and adds one MCP owner that captures every seat's authored version through the existing per-deck capture owner and builds the admission. Construction legality stays with MCP per deck; executability stays with the worker's qualifier; nothing merges decks or chooses a printing for another seat.

## Ownership and reuse review

- **One capture owner.** Every deck in a pod is captured by `captureCatalog`, the existing per-deck owner over the deck store, the pinned index read, the served snapshot label, the identity authority and `validateDeck`; the pod owner adds nothing to that verdict and never substitutes a deck. A seat the owner refuses refuses the whole pod with that seat's code.
- **Immutable inputs, reused.** A capture is an authored deck version's bytes and digest; a version several seats play is captured once and named by each of them, and the same deck at two versions may sit in one pod. The pod digest orders the captures, so a reordered pod is another admission.
- **Transfer integrity in the worker, construction legality in MCP.** The worker verifies each capture as `catalog_check` does and then only that the captures agree (one served snapshot, no repeats, equal shared records, no printing owned by two identities). It resolves no name, merges no deck and judges no construction. Two seats sharing an Oracle identity, or a whole deck, hold distinct physical cards the core's setup owner allocates from shared definitions.
- **Executability unchanged.** Qualification still runs once per distinct Oracle identity through `cards::support` under the Commander profile; the unsupported command-zone variants (partner, background, Doctor's companion, two commanders, meld, a companion) stay refused, now per refusing capture with the deck named. No real deck reaches setup until the qualifier supports whole cards.
- **Single-capture preflight untouched.** `catalog_check`, `support_check` and `profile_preflight` keep `ProfileCatalog`; the evidence workflow's per-card checks are unchanged.
- **Contract discipline.** The wire shape changed, so the protocol version moved with regenerated bindings and fixtures on both sides; the schema digest negotiation rejects mixed artifacts. The frame bound moved with the pod so the contract admits nothing it cannot transport.
- **No MCP tool yet.** The pod owner feeds the worker protocol directly and is exercised through the pinned binary; exposing it as an MCP route belongs to the cutover and workflow units once a real deck can be admitted.
- **Overlap.** None added. The preview path is untouched apart from where it reads its catalog digest.

## Limits

- No real Commander deck can be admitted or run until the qualifier supports whole cards (`engine-mvp-corpus`, `engine-mvp-support-runtime`); mixed pods are proven through the procedure with test-built definitions and refused per card through the pinned binary.
- The pod owner is not an MCP route; `engine-mvp-cutover` and `engine-mvp-mcp-workflow` expose it once a deck can be admitted.
- Partner, background, Doctor's companion, two commanders, a meld commander and a companion stay refused as unsupported; their runtime semantics belong to the keyword and lifecycle units.
- Every seat's physical ids are assigned by the MCP pod owner; the engine's setup inventory remains the one correspondence between them and engine objects.
- A pod is bound to one served snapshot and index revision; decks captured against different snapshots must be recaptured together.
