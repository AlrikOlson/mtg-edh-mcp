# Starting procedure: shuffle, opening hands, mulligans and the first draw

Accepted for `engine-mvp-mulligans` under the [engine charter](../ENGINE-CHARTER.md). The research finding and plan are `think:432` and `think:433`; native Magistr records hold execution status. It closes the starting-procedure hook the [Commander unit](engine-mvp-commander.md) left open and the first-turn draw finding the [multiplayer unit](engine-multiplayer.md) recorded.

The [source pin](../../engine/source.json) advances to canonical `mtg-game-engine` commit `3049cf95d0ba2db5b10d9c2aedfd4feb2fa6dce6` on `main`. The root `Cargo.lock` changes only by the desktop crate dropping `rand` and `rand_chacha`; the worker contract schema is unchanged, so only the synced README and the manifest's source revision move.

## Scope

Before this unit no owner shuffled a library, dealt a hand or took a mulligan. `setup::build_game` placed the hand it was given, the worker's Commander admission translated a qualified pod to command-zone blueprints with empty hands and then refused it with `starting_procedure_unavailable`, the desktop shuffled and dealt each seat with its own ChaCha stream before calling setup, and every game built through setup had the first seat skip its turn-one draw regardless of seat count. This unit adds the one owner, routes the worker and the desktop through it, and makes setup decide the first-draw rule from the seat count.

## Ownership and reuse review

- **One owner per decision.** Shuffling, dealing, mulligan rounds, bottoming and the first-draw rule are decided in the core (`starting.rs` and `setup.rs`). The worker translates and retains; the desktop calls the owner; neither orders a card. The shuffle draws from `GameState::rng_mut`, the same ChaCha stream the rest of the game uses, and reorders through the zone manager's existing `reorder_zone_for_owner`; draws go through `turn::step_actions::draw_card`, the draw step's own owner.
- **Retired duplicate.** The desktop's `seat_blueprint` shuffle and deal were the last production ordering outside the core. They are gone; the desktop's `rand` and `rand_chacha` dependencies with them.
- **Hidden information.** A client's whole surface is `StartingObservation`; it carries no library and no other player's hand. The record keeps bottomed card identities for replay consumers, marked as hidden information to scope like the library they describe.
- **Contract unchanged.** Mulligans taken and opening hand sizes are recorded in the core and not reported on the wire, and a keep or bottom decision cannot be relayed to a parent, because both change the schema hash and no production pod can reach the seam yet (the qualifier still refuses every card under the Commander profile). Both obligations are filed on the new pending chunk `engine-mvp-starting-report`.
- **Overlap.** None added. The preview path is untouched: the fixture still deals as it declares and the six-card seats cannot exceed seven.

## Rules basis

From the engine's retained Comprehensive Rules snapshot: 103.3 (each player shuffles; the deck becomes the library), 103.5 (draw seven; the starting player declares first, then turn order; all mulligans taken at once; a mulligan shuffles the hand into the library, draws seven and bottoms as many cards as mulligans taken in any order; a player may take mulligans until the opening hand would be zero), 103.5c (the first mulligan does not count in a multiplayer game), 103.8a and 103.8c (the starting player skips the first draw only in a two-player game), 903.6 and 903.7 (the commander apart in the command zone; 40 life; seven cards).

## Limits

- No real Commander deck can be admitted or run until the qualifier supports whole cards (`engine-mvp-corpus`, `engine-mvp-support-runtime`); the session's Commander retention is therefore exercised in production only once a deck qualifies, and its pieces are proven individually (the admission owner, the retain helper through the preview path, the trial setup through the driver).
- Mulligans taken, opening hand sizes and final-hand outcomes are not reported on the wire, and a relay seat keeps every hand: `engine-mvp-starting-report`.
- The legacy `simulate_deck` keep and mulligan labels remain heuristics until `engine-mvp-cutover` switches the route to engine evidence; they do not simulate this procedure.
- Actions from the opening hand (CR 103.6), starting hand size modifiers (CR 103.5a) and a chosen starting player (CR 103.1) are not modeled; the first seat is the starting player.
- The baseline's starting client is a fixed land-count rule with no measured strength (`engine-mvp-policies`).
