## Rules basis

From the pinned Comprehensive Rules corpus (SHA-256 `4381ad1b…`, effective 2026-08-07): CR 702.21a defines ward as a triggered ability, "Whenever this permanent becomes the target of a spell or ability an opponent controls, counter that spell or ability unless that player pays [cost]"; CR 700.14 defines expend as paying a cost to cast a spell such that the mana that player spent this turn to cast spells was less than N before that payment and at least N after it; CR 601.2h pays the total cost during casting; CR 608.2 governs resolution. Together these fix the semantics implemented here: the targeting player, not the ward's controller, decides at resolution; a ward whose spell or ability already left the stack has nothing to counter; only the final cost's actual mana counts toward expend, never activation fees, life or printed mana value; and a threshold is crossed at most once per turn per player.

## Independent evidence

Canonical `tests/ward_expend.rs` holds seventeen scenarios written before the implementation compiled (nineteen compile errors naming exactly the absent API). Ward: an opponent's targeting fires the trigger and the controller's own targeting does not, while both are observable events; paying mana taps the payer's lands, keeps the spell, and the spell then resolves; declining counters the spell into its owner's graveyard; declining counters an activated ability and leaves its tapped source; a ward whose spell was countered first asks for nothing and taps nothing; a life ward at exactly enough life pays to zero and one life short offers only `No`; a discard ward routes through the shared `Objects` request, rejects a foreign answer, and offers only `No` on an empty hand; a combined mana-plus-discard ward pays nothing before the selection and both together after it; an unaffordable mana ward offers only `No`; foreign, forged and stale answers preserve bytes and events; `WardCost` admits only mana, life and discard and rejects X. Expend: base, kicker and a registered reduction total 1, 5 and 8 with exact `ManaSpentCasting` events and no event for a `{0}` spell; an activated-ability fee, a mana-ability fee and a Phyrexian life payment are excluded; `Expend { 8 }` fires exactly once when 5 then 4 crosses eight, an opponent's 8 does not, and 3 more does not retrigger; a later observer is not triggered retroactively; the untap step resets both players while advancing steps alone does not; a restored snapshot preserves the counter and both the exact hash and Zobrist distinguish totals.

Four temporary mutations each fail at least one scenario: routing the request to the ward's controller fails eight; removing the departed-object guard fails the countered-spell scenario; recording the adjusted nominal value plus life instead of the actual spend fails the exclusion scenario; resetting only the active player fails the every-player reset scenario.

## Boundaries and retained work

Ward costs are engine definitions supplied by a card program; binding `Ward {3}` from Oracle text to `Card.ward`, registering the keyword trigger when the permanent enters, and ward with X (CR 702.21b), hybrid, Phyrexian or sacrifice costs remain with the keyword batch and card-program owners. The engine still registers no card's triggered abilities with the event bus outside tests and mana sources, casting emits no `SpellCast` event, and countering or resolving emits no stack-departure zone event; these are recorded on their owning chunks and one pending chunk now names the event gap. Expend only counts mana paid through the canonical casting transaction; Trailtracker Scout's return-target effect and Kairi's dies trigger remain with corpus effects. The unified decision surface (`engine-mvp-decisions`) can now treat ward payment and expend as closed prerequisites of the cost-choices aggregate.
